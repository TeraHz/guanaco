## 3. Full Description (Max 4,000 characters)

```markdown
Guanaco is an open-source mobile client for Vikunja, the self-hosted task management platform.

It connects directly to your own Vikunja instance, letting you manage your to-do lists and projects from your phone.

Features:
• Tasks & Lists: View, create, edit, and complete tasks organized by project.
• Task Details: Manage subtasks, due dates, priorities, labels, and notes.
• Offline Support: Work offline with local storage; changes sync back to your server when you reconnect.
• Quick Input: Simple text shortcuts for quickly adding tags (#), priorities (!), and dates.
• Privacy: Guanaco connects only to your configured Vikunja server. It contains no analytics, no ads, and no third-party tracking.
• Biometrics: Optional biometric lock to protect app access on your device.

Requirements:
Guanaco is a client app and requires an accessible Vikunja server (self-hosted or hosted). It does not provide standalone task storage without a server.

Open Source:

Source code: https://github.com/TeraHz/guanaco
```
