Privacy Policy for Guanaco

Effective Date: October 4, 2026 | Last Updated: November 23, 2026

Guanaco is a free, open-source mobile and tablet app for Vikunja. Your tasks and personal information belong solely to you.

Guanaco does not collect, track, store on developer servers, or sell any personal data, tasks, or usage statistics.

1. What We Never Collect

  • No Analytics or Crash Trackers: Guanaco contains no analytics, telemetry, or crash-reporting services.
  • No Ads: Guanaco displays no advertisements and never accesses advertising identifiers.
  • No Intermediate Servers: Your tasks, notes, labels, and login details are never routed through developer servers or third-party push-notification relays.
  • No Saved Passwords: Your Vikunja password (and two-factor code, if enabled) is used once to sign in and is never saved on your device.

2. How Data Is Stored on Your Device

  • Direct Connection to Your Server: All syncing happens strictly between your device and the Vikunja server address you enter. Connections use encrypted HTTPS by default (connecting over unencrypted local HTTP requires your confirmation).
  • Encrypted Session Storage: Your active login token and username are stored in your device's encrypted system keychain/keystore.
  • Local Offline Copy: Tasks and unsent offline edits are stored locally inside the app's private storage so you can work without an internet connection. Automatic cloud app-data backups are disabled on Android so your cached tasks are not uploaded to third-party backup services.
  • On-Device Grouping: Smart task grouping (such as shopping aisles) runs locally on your phone — task titles are never sent to external AI services.
  • App Updates (Google Play): Play Store builds use the standard Google Play update check solely to notify you when a newer version of Guanaco is available.

3. Device Permissions

  • Network Access: Used to sync directly with your Vikunja server and detect when you go offline or reconnect.
  • Notifications (Optional): Used only to show task reminders scheduled locally on your device, and to restore pending reminders after your phone restarts.
  • Biometrics (Optional): Used only when you turn on Biometric Lock (Fingerprint or Face ID). Unlock checks are handled by your device's operating system; Guanaco never sees or stores biometric data.
  • Vibration: Used for subtle touch feedback when completing or swiping tasks.

4. Deleting Your Data

Because we run no servers for Guanaco, we never hold a copy of your data. Tapping Log Out or uninstalling the app removes your login session, cached tasks, and scheduled reminders from your device. Data stored on your Vikunja server remains under the control of your Vikunja server administrator.
